    Get started

    Mods for your M8, straight over USB

    New effects, modulation and tools for the Dirtywave M8. Pick a mod, press Install, and the page copies it onto your M8's card over USB. Your M8 loads it at the next restart.

    • Only your M8: no account, nothing uploaded anywhere
    • Each mod is checked by its sha256 before it goes on the card
    • Switch any mod off from here or on the M8

    1. 1Put dirtyloader core on your M8

      Once, like a firmware update: core is what loads mods from the card. Model:02 6.6.4A or 6.6.2A and Headless 6.6.4A, built and flashed from this page.

      How to put core on your M8

    2. 2Connect it

      Plug the M8 in with USB, close m8c, and press Connect M8. Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer.

    3. 3Install mods, then restart

      Press + Install on the mods you want. Restart the M8 from here when you're done. All four directions at once (UP+DOWN+LEFT+RIGHT) on a Model:02 open the MOD LOADER; on a Headless, LEFT+RIGHT opens the mods' pages.

    Browse the mods

    Every mod here is built from the dirtyloader repository's examples, and the page checks each file by its sha256 before it sends it.

            Get started
            
      
            How to use dirtyloader
            
      
            dirtyloader has two parts. Core is a small addition to the M8's firmware: you put it
        on once, like a firmware update. Mods then go on the SD card from the Mod Shop, as often as you
        like. This page builds core into Dirtywave's own firmware file and flashes it, here in this tab.
            
      
              
        
            • Your firmware file never leaves this tab
              • 
        
            • No Dirtywave firmware is hosted here: you bring their file
              • 
        
            • Dirtywave's file always takes your M8 back to stock
              •

            Recover
            
      
            Getting out of trouble
            
      
            From a mod that misbehaves to going all the way back to Dirtywave's firmware. Your songs, samples
        and settings stay on the card through all of it.
            
    
            
    
            
      
            
        
            A mod misbehaves
            
        
            Switch it off on On your M8, or in the MOD LOADER on the M8 (all four directions; SHIFT+EDIT on a mod), and restart.
          Or restart once with no mods at all:
            
        
            
          
          
        
            
        
            Needs the M8 connected here.
            
      
            
      
            
        
            It crashes or hangs while starting
            
        
            Core guards the start. If the last start with mods crashed, it starts once without them by itself. If it
          hung, switch the M8 off and on: a start that didn't run for 10 s also skips the mods next time. The MODS
          page then says SKIPPED. Switch off the mod at fault, then press Load mods again here, or
          RESTART on the MODS page.
            
      
            
      
            
        
            Back to Dirtywave's firmware
            
        
            Flash Dirtywave's file as it is. Their own ways work too: the SD card's FIRMWARE UPDATE (Model:02) or
          TyUploader. Or flash it from here, with the same steps as Get started:
            
        
            
          
          Drop Dirtywave's .bin (Model:02), .hex (Headless) or their .zip, or choose it
        
            
        
        
            
        
            
          
          
        
            
        
            
      
            
      
            
        
            The M8 doesn't start at all
            
        
            The bootloader is in the Teensy's own chip and nothing here writes it, so the M8 can always be flashed again.
          On a Headless, press the button on the Teensy: it goes into its bootloader. Then use
          It's already in its bootloader above, or TyUploader. On a Model:02,
          TyUploader or Dirtywave's support can take you through it.
            
      
            
      
            
        
            Remove dirtyloader's files
            
        
            Everything core and the mods keep is in /dirtyloader on the card, plus a .mods
          (and .dldata) file beside each song saved with mods. Stock firmware ignores them; delete them
          with a card reader if you like.
            
      
            
      
            
        
            The theme
            
        
            SYSTEM SETTINGS > THEME on the M8 loads any theme. Core's extra colours switch off on the
          Theme page, and stock firmware doesn't use them.

            About
            
  
            dirtyloader puts mods on a Dirtywave M8. Its core, a small addition to the M8's firmware, loads the mods it finds on
    the SD card (/dirtyloader/mods) at boot. This page installs them over USB with Web Serial: it talks to
    core the same way python -m dirtyloader.unit does.
            
  
            Get started builds core into Dirtywave's firmware file in this tab and flashes it over USB
    (WebHID, the Teensy's own bootloader). The page holds a patch per firmware version, not firmware: only bytes
    that come from dirtyloader, checked by sha256 against your file and the result.
            
  
            Going back
            
  
              
    
            • A mod misbehaves: switch it off here or in the MOD LOADER (all four directions), and restart. If the M8
      crashes at boot, core boots once without mods by itself.
              • 
    
            • Stock firmware: Dirtywave's own file, flashed from Recover, the SD
      card or TyUploader. Your songs and settings stay.
              • 
    
            • Everything else: Recover.
